## How to read the table

- **Kev rank:** the question's place among the 40 Kev reads (C2, C3, CTRL and C1a-40 read the same 40), in the order Kev sees them. Blank: not among the 40.
- **Precision, recall, F0.5 (train):** treating a *yes* answer as a prediction of success, on the training founders. 20% of founders succeed, so a precision above 0.20 means a yes makes success more likely. Terms: [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md).
- **C1a weight, C1a-40 weight:** the elastic net's weight on the question's P(yes) (positive: a yes raises P(success)). Blank: weight zero, so the question isn't used.
- **Vote:** ✓ if the paper's vote (C1b) counts it.
- **Near-duplicates:** TRL keeps questions that ask nearly the same thing (for example ranks 3–5, all about an acquired prior startup). Their answers move together, so the elastic net splits weight between them, and one twin can get a negative weight that offsets another's positive one. Read near-duplicates' weights together, not one by one.
